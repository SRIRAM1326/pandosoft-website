An assisted approach to OTC digital asset transactions.

PANDOSOFT INDIA helps eligible customers discuss and arrange supported OTC digital asset transactions. Each request is assessed against the customer’s approved profile, service availability and applicable checks.

1. Tell us what you need

Share the asset, intended transaction direction, approximate amount and preferred timeframe. Our team will confirm whether the requirement can be supported and what information is needed.

2. Review commercial terms

Before proceeding, review the quoted price, quantity, fees, network, quote validity and settlement arrangements. Do not transfer funds or assets solely on the basis of an indicative website price or an unverified message.

3. Confirm settlement

Use only the account and wallet instructions confirmed through our designated process. Check the beneficiary, asset, network and wallet address carefully. An incorrect blockchain transfer may be irreversible.

4. Keep transaction records

Retain the applicable quote, acceptance, payment references and transaction confirmations for your records.

Service availability: Supported assets, networks, minimum amounts, transaction limits and timelines are confirmed for each request. We do not guarantee that every enquiry can be accepted or executed.

Corporate Document Checklist

Requirements depend on your profile. The portal specifies exact documentation needed for approval:

  • Certificate of Incorporation (COI) / Partnership deed
  • Entity PAN card and GSTIN registration certificate
  • Board Resolution / Authorization Letter designating corporate signers
  • Identity & address proof of authorized signers & beneficial owners
  • Corporate bank account statement or cancelled cheque
  • Whitelisted non-custodial destination wallet address

Two-Hour Payment Cooling Period

Important clarification: The two-hour payment cooling period applies to transaction funding; it is separate from wallet activation and is not a two-hour end-to-end settlement guarantee.

  • Applies upon incoming banking wire credit to verify wire authenticity.
  • Operates completely separately from the 24-hour wallet activation lock.
  • Protects against payment recall and fraud.
  • Settlement proceeds promptly after banking clearance and compliance verification.

Verify before you transfer.

Never share credentials

Never share your account password, OTP, private key or wallet recovery phrase. PANDOSOFT will never ask for them under any circumstance.

Official email domain verification

Check that emails come from @pandosoftindia.com. PANDOSOFT does not use generic email addresses such as Gmail, Yahoo, or Outlook for official correspondence.

Confirm banking details and wallet addresses through your approved portal channel before transferring funds. Do not rely on unverified messages, screenshots or third-party payment requests.

Beware of unrealistic promises

Be cautious of anyone offering guaranteed returns, urgent action or private trading arrangements, or asking for an unlock fee.

If you notice suspicious activity or believe you have received an unauthorized communication, contact Admin@pandosoftindia.com immediately.

Questions & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions.
Proposed public answers.

Clear guidance on our assisted OTC digital asset desk, compliance, quotes, and execution.

Contact the OTC Desk
What is OTC crypto trading?

OTC means over-the-counter. It involves a transaction arranged directly with a service provider rather than an order placed on a public exchange order book. Pricing and settlement terms are agreed for the proposed transaction.

Is PANDOSOFT INDIA a self-service trading exchange?

PANDOSOFT provides OTC services through online company onboarding and a dedicated customer portal for purchase requests, rate updates, transaction updates and withdrawal requests. The portal supports an OTC service workflow; it is not a public exchange order book.

Who can apply?

Companies can apply online. Eligibility depends on the company profile, verification and acceptance under our policies. Contact us to discuss your business requirement.

How do I onboard?

Select Start Onboarding and complete the steps in our onboarding portal. If you need help, email Admin@pandosoftindia.com.

What documents are needed?

Requirements depend on your profile. You may be asked for identity, address, tax, bank account and source-of-funds information. Businesses may also need incorporation, ownership and authorised signatory documents. The portal and review team will specify the applicable requirements.

Which assets and networks are supported?

USDT is our primary OTC focus. Other digital assets may be supported based on client requirements and availability. Always confirm the exact asset, blockchain network and wallet address for each transaction.

What is the minimum transaction amount?

Contact Info@pandosoftindia.com for the minimum applicable to your proposed transaction. Any threshold will be confirmed before proceeding.

How are rates and charges calculated?

Rates depend on the asset, quantity, liquidity and market conditions. Review your transaction-specific quote for the price, charges, validity and settlement terms. Do not assume that a separate service fee is the only cost; review the total consideration.

How long do onboarding and settlement take?

Compliance review depends on document completeness and any additional checks. Following approval, allow 24 hours to activate the wallet address supplied during onboarding, then complete the required test transaction. For regular transactions, allow a two-hour cooling period for funds to reflect in PANDOSOFT’s account. Same-day service is available for eligible approved transactions, subject to banking confirmation, checks, liquidity and network conditions.

Can I transact before onboarding approval?

Transactions can proceed only after the necessary customer and transaction checks and approvals. An enquiry or application does not authorise a transfer of funds or assets.

Can I pay from someone else’s account?

Do not use a third-party account unless expressly permitted under the applicable policy and confirmed in writing through the designated process. Confirm the permitted funding account before paying.

Can a transaction be cancelled?

Cancellation depends on the transaction stage and applicable terms. Once accepted or executed, a transaction may not be cancellable. Blockchain transfers may be irreversible. Review the cancellation and refund policy before accepting a quote.

Does a stablecoin eliminate risk?

No. Stablecoins can involve issuer, reserve, de-pegging, liquidity, operational and blockchain risks. A stated price peg does not guarantee value or redemption.
