An assisted approach to OTC digital asset transactions.

PANDOSOFT INDIA helps eligible customers discuss and arrange supported OTC digital asset transactions. Each request is assessed against the customer’s approved profile, service availability and applicable checks.

1. Tell us what you need

Share the asset, intended transaction direction, approximate amount and preferred timeframe. Our team will confirm whether the requirement can be supported and what information is needed.

2. Review commercial terms

Before proceeding, review the quoted price, quantity, fees, network, quote validity and settlement arrangements. Do not transfer funds or assets solely on the basis of an indicative website price or an unverified message.

3. Confirm settlement

Use only the account and wallet instructions confirmed through our designated process. Check the beneficiary, asset, network and wallet address carefully. An incorrect blockchain transfer may be irreversible.

4. Keep transaction records

Retain the applicable quote, acceptance, payment references and transaction confirmations for your records.

Service availability: Supported assets, networks, minimum amounts, transaction limits and timelines are confirmed for each request. We do not guarantee that every enquiry can be accepted or executed.

Is trading crypto legal in India?

Yes. Virtual digital assets are legal to buy, sell and hold, and crypto derivatives are permitted on registered platforms. PandoSoft is registered with the Financial Intelligence Unit as a reporting VASP, applies full KYC, and files the prescribed reports. Gains are taxed at 30% plus surcharge and cess, and 1% TDS applies on every transfer — we deduct it and report it for you.

How much do I need to start?

₹100 is enough for a spot purchase. There is no minimum balance, no account fee and no inactivity charge. Futures positions start at a ₹500 margin on BTC/INR, which is the smallest contract size we offer.

What are the actual fees?

Spot is a flat 0.10% taker and 0.05% maker. Perpetual futures are 0.03% taker and 0.015% maker. INR deposits and withdrawals are free on UPI, NEFT and IMPS. The only other statutory charge is the 1% TDS on sells, which is not ours to keep.

How is my money and custody handled?

Rupee balances sit in segregated escrow accounts with scheduled commercial banks. 95% of customer coins are held in offline multi-signature cold wallets, and we publish a monthly proof-of-reserves attestation from an independent auditor. We currently hold 104.6% of customer liabilities.

Can I withdraw whenever I want?

Yes. INR withdrawals process same-day on NEFT and IMPS, with a median of 11 minutes. Crypto withdrawals go to an allowlisted address after a 24-hour lock on newly added addresses. There is no lock-in, no staking requirement and no withdrawal cap tied to your trading volume.

What happens to my taxes at the end of the year?

Every sell, spend and swap is logged with its TDS entry. At any point you can export a complete VDA transaction statement as CSV or PDF, formatted for the Schedule VDA in your ITR, and hand it straight to your chartered accountant.
