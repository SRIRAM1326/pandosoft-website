An assisted approach to OTC digital asset transactions.

PANDOSOFT INDIA helps eligible customers discuss and arrange supported OTC digital asset transactions. Each request is assessed against the customer’s approved profile, service availability and applicable checks.

1. Tell us what you need

Share the asset, intended transaction direction, approximate amount and preferred timeframe. Our team will confirm whether the requirement can be supported and what information is needed.

2. Review commercial terms

Before proceeding, review the quoted price, quantity, fees, network, quote validity and settlement arrangements. Do not transfer funds or assets solely on the basis of an indicative website price or an unverified message.

3. Confirm settlement

Use only the account and wallet instructions confirmed through our designated process. Check the beneficiary, asset, network and wallet address carefully. An incorrect blockchain transfer may be irreversible.

4. Keep transaction records

Retain the applicable quote, acceptance, payment references and transaction confirmations for your records.

Service availability: Supported assets, networks, minimum amounts, transaction limits and timelines are confirmed for each request. We do not guarantee that every enquiry can be accepted or executed.

Security & Fraud Awareness

Verify before you transfer.

Security begins with caution and verification. PANDOSOFT follows strict security protocols and urges every customer to stay vigilant against unauthorized communications and fraud.

Official Security Notice

Never Share Credentials

PANDOSOFT representatives will never request your account password, OTP, private keys, or wallet recovery phrase.

Report Suspicious Contact →

Never share credentials

Never share your account password, OTP, private key or wallet recovery phrase. PANDOSOFT will never ask for them under any circumstance.

Official email domain verification

Check that emails come from @pandosoftindia.com. PANDOSOFT does not use generic email addresses such as Gmail, Yahoo, or Outlook for official correspondence.

Confirm banking & wallet details

Confirm banking details and wallet addresses through your approved portal channel before transferring funds. Do not rely on unverified messages, screenshots or third-party payment requests.

Beware of unrealistic promises

Be cautious of anyone offering guaranteed returns, urgent action or private trading arrangements, or asking for an unlock fee.

Immediate fraud reporting

If you notice suspicious activity or believe you have received an unauthorized communication, contact support@pandosoftindia.com immediately.

Verification before transactions

Transactions can proceed only after the necessary customer and transaction checks and approvals. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or execution.

Pricing

Know the terms before you confirm.

OTC pricing depends on the asset, transaction size, available liquidity and market conditions. Your quote will set out the applicable price, charges and settlement terms for review before acceptance.

Quotes are time-sensitive. If a quote expires or the requirement changes, a revised quote may be needed. Applicable taxes or deductions will be addressed in the transaction documentation.
