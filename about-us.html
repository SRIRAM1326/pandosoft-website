About Us

Meet PANDOSOFT INDIA.

PANDOSOFT INDIA is the trade name of PandoSoft India Private Limited. Our current service focus is OTC digital asset transactions, supported by online customer onboarding and direct communication about transaction requirements.

Our approach

Our operational philosophy is built on three foundational pillars designed to ensure transparency, compliance, and seamless execution.

Clarity before commitment

Customers should understand the proposed transaction and its terms before confirming.

Information that supports review

Accurate customer details and appropriate documentation form part of the onboarding process.

Communication throughout the process

We aim to make requirements and next steps clear, from the initial enquiry through transaction coordination.

Our website presents services currently available. Any additional services will be announced with their own eligibility requirements and terms when launched.
