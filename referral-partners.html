Responsibilities & Boundaries

You introduce. PANDOSOFT manages the OTC service.

As a Referral Partner, your role is to introduce interested businesses. PANDOSOFT manages customer onboarding, compliance review, quotation and transaction support through its designated process.

Important Boundary Notice: Referral Partner status does not authorise you to collect customer funds, accept digital assets, approve accounts, confirm quotes or make commitments on PANDOSOFT’s behalf. Customers must transact exclusively through PANDOSOFT’s official channels.

Partner Q&A

Referral Program FAQs.

Answers to common questions regarding eligibility, commissions, and partner conduct.

Can an individual apply?

Yes. Individuals can submit an application. Participation is subject to PANDOSOFT’s review and acceptance of the program terms.

Does referring a company guarantee approval?

No. Every referred company must complete onboarding and the applicable compliance review. A referral does not bypass verification.

When do I earn a commission?

When a referred customer completes a qualifying transaction and all eligibility conditions in your approved program terms are met. A submitted lead or onboarding application alone does not establish an earned commission.

How much can I earn?

The commission arrangement is confirmed in your approved partner terms. Earnings depend on eligible customer activity; no fixed income is guaranteed.

Do I receive commissions on every future transaction?

Only transactions within the scope and earning duration stated in your approved terms qualify. Ongoing or lifetime entitlement should not be assumed.

Can I refer an existing PANDOSOFT customer?

Existing customers and duplicate referrals are handled under the program’s attribution rules. Confirm eligibility before assuming a referral qualifies.

Can I collect payment from a referred customer?

No. Customers must use the payment and wallet instructions provided through PANDOSOFT’s designated transaction process.

How do I get help?

Email Info@pandosoftindia.com or call/WhatsApp +91 96297 77927, Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–6:30 PM IST. Existing approved partners should use the support channel specified in their agreement.
